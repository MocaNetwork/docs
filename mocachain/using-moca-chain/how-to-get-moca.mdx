&#36;MOCA is the network token used to pay for transactions on Moca Chain.
Learn more about the [&#36;MOCA token](/learn/moca-coin).

## Mainnet

### Bridge from Ethereum or Base

You can bridge your existing &#36;MOCA from Ethereum or Base to Moca Chain with [Stargate](https://stargate.finance), powered by LayerZero. Bridging works in both directions, so you can also move &#36;MOCA from Moca Chain back to Ethereum or Base.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Go to [stargate.finance](https://stargate.finance) and connect your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the route">
    Select &#36;MOCA on Ethereum or Base as the source, and Moca Chain as the destination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the transfer">
    Enter the amount, review the fee, and confirm the transaction in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive on Moca Chain">
    Your &#36;MOCA arrives on Moca Chain within a few minutes. You can track the transfer on [LayerZero Scan](https://layerzeroscan.com).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Fees:** You pay a LayerZero messaging fee in the source chain's gas token: ETH when you bridge from Ethereum or Base, and &#36;MOCA when you bridge out of Moca Chain. Stargate shows the fee before you confirm. Bridging from Moca Chain to Ethereum costs more than to Base because the fee covers Ethereum gas.

**Daily limits:** Each route has a daily limit that resets at 00:00 UTC. If your transfer doesn't go through, try a smaller amount or try again the next day.

<Note>
When you bridge out of Moca Chain, some wallets label the amount as ETH on the confirmation screen. The amount is in &#36;MOCA and includes the bridge fee.
</Note>

To check that you are using the official &#36;MOCA token, compare the contract address:

| Network    | &#36;MOCA contract                              |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Ethereum   | `0xF944e35f95E819E752f3cCB5Faf40957d311e8c5` |
| Base       | `0x2B11834Ed1FeAEd4b4b3a86A6F571315E25A884D` |
| Moca Chain | Native token (no contract address)          |

### Available on Exchanges

Currently &#36;MOCA is available on various centralized exchanges. For more information please refer to [Coin Gecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/moca-coin)

## Testnet

Go to the [**Testnet Faucet**](https://faucet.mocachain.org).

1. Verify on Discord (this is done to reduce botting)
2. Then enter your address then click Request

> _Note that you can only request from our faucet once every 24 hours._
