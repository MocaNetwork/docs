AIR Agentic Money is the transaction authority for agents. It controls what an agent can spend, authorize, hold, route, or settle on behalf of a user.

<Info>
**The core question.** Can the policy engine block a $5/day agent from betting on Polymarket while still allowing it to buy ETH?
</Info>

## Graduated autonomy

AIR Agentic Money supports three levels of authority. Users move up the ladder as trust grows; partners and merchants pick the level appropriate to the task.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Full account operation — human in the loop">
    The agent prepares the transaction, but every payment requires explicit user confirmation. Used for first-time merchants, large amounts, or high-risk categories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sub-accounts with delegated budgets">
    The user provisions a sub-account or burner card with a fixed balance. The agent transacts freely within the sub-account; the main account is never touched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action-restricted spending — policy engine allowlists / denylists">
    The agent transacts directly under a spend mandate. The policy engine evaluates each request against budgets, merchant scope, category scope, expiry, and approval thresholds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What AIR Agentic Money controls

- **Spend mandate** — bounded transaction authority granted to an agent.
- **Budget cap** — total amount cap, per-transaction cap, or recurring cap.
- **Merchant scope** — allowlist of merchants the mandate applies to.
- **Category scope** — allowlist of merchant categories (e.g. office supplies, travel, groceries).
- **Expiry** — time-bound authority.
- **Approval threshold** — amount above which user confirmation is required.
- **Wallet / account reference** — which AIR Account & Money wallet, sub-account, or instrument funds the spend.
- **Sub-account / burner card** — isolated balance or virtual card bound to the mandate.
- **Payment authorization** — the policy-approved authorization to initiate a payment.
- **Rail selection** — which payment rail or framework adapter is used.
- **Receipt** — user-visible and machine-readable evidence.
- **Refund / dispute** — the path for reversing a settled payment.

## A spend mandate in plain terms

A spend mandate is the contract between user and agent for what the agent is allowed to pay for. It might say:

> Up to **$50** with **`merchant_abc`**, in the **office supplies** category, expiring **Friday at midnight**, with user approval required for any single charge above **$25**.

When the agent later requests a payment, AIR Policy + Audit checks the mandate, and AIR Agentic Money returns an authorization, denial, or "approval required" response — with a receipt for every outcome.

## What an agent receives, and what it never receives

  <div>

## How agent relationships use Agentic Money

| Relationship | Typical money pattern |
| --- | --- |
| **Butler** | Bounded spend mandates, delegated sub-accounts, persistent payment authority within budget. |
| **Concierge** | Merchant-bound, single-purpose payment authorization for the merchant's product or service. |
| **Platform** | Routing of payment intent, proof of payment capability, escrow instructions, settlement metadata. |

## Working with rails and frameworks

AIR Agentic Money does not replace existing payment rails. It composes with x402, AP2, Stripe ACP / SPT, Visa TAP, Mastercard Agent Pay / KYA, and MPP / Tempo through framework adapters in AIR Kit.

See [Existing frameworks](/airai/concepts/frameworks) for the full mapping.
