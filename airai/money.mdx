AIR Money (Agentic) is the transaction authority for agents. It controls what an agent
can spend, authorize, hold, route, or settle on behalf of a user.

<Info>
**The core question.** Can the policy engine stop a \$5/day agent from spending on an
out-of-scope merchant, while still letting it renew a subscription or top up a
stablecoin balance?
</Info>

## Graduated autonomy

AIR Money (Agentic) supports three levels of authority. Users move up the ladder as trust
grows; partners and merchants pick the level appropriate to the task.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Full account operation — human in the loop">
    The agent prepares the transaction, but every payment requires explicit user
    confirmation. Used for first-time merchants, large amounts, or high-risk categories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sub-accounts with delegated budgets">
    The user provisions a sub-account or burner card with a fixed balance. The agent
    transacts freely within the sub-account; the main account is never touched.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action-restricted spending — policy engine allowlists / denylists">
    The agent transacts directly under a spend mandate. The policy engine evaluates each
    request against budgets, merchant scope, category scope, expiry, and approval thresholds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What AIR Money (Agentic) controls

- **Spend mandate** — bounded transaction authority granted to an agent.
- **Budget cap** — total amount cap, per-transaction cap, or recurring cap.
- **Merchant scope** — allowlist of merchants the mandate applies to.
- **Category scope** — allowlist of merchant categories (e.g. office supplies, travel, groceries).
- **Expiry** — time-bound authority.
- **Approval threshold** — amount above which user confirmation is required.
- **Wallet / account reference** — which AIR Account & Money wallet, sub-account, or instrument funds the spend.
- **Sub-account / burner card** — isolated balance or virtual card bound to the mandate.
- **Payment authorization** — the policy-approved authorization to initiate a payment.
- **Rail selection** — which payment rail or framework adapter is used.
- **Receipt** — user-visible and machine-readable evidence.
- **Refund / dispute** — the path for reversing a settled payment.

## A spend mandate in plain terms

A spend mandate is the contract between user and agent for what the agent is allowed to
pay for. It might say:

> Up to **\$50** with **`merchant_abc`**, in the **office supplies** category, expiring
> **Friday at midnight**, with user approval required for any single charge above **\$25**.

When the agent later requests a payment, AIR Policy + Audit checks the mandate, and AIR
Money (Agentic) returns an authorization, denial, or "approval required" response — with
a receipt for every outcome.

## What an agent receives, and what it never receives

<Columns cols={2}>
  <div>
    <Check>A spend mandate it can use within scope</Check>
    <Check>A payment authorization for an in-scope request</Check>
    <Check>A merchant-bound payment instrument or token</Check>
    <Check>A receipt for every successful or failed payment</Check>
    <Check>A refund or dispute reference if needed</Check>
  </div>
  <div>
    <Warning>Wallet signing keys</Warning>
    <Warning>Card numbers or rail credentials</Warning>
    <Warning>Unrestricted account access</Warning>
    <Warning>Authority outside the mandate scope</Warning>
    <Warning>Persistent payment authority after expiry or revocation</Warning>
  </div>

## Working with rails and frameworks

AIR Money (Agentic) does not replace existing payment rails. It composes with x402, AP2,
Stripe ACP / SPT, Visa TAP, Mastercard Agent Pay / KYA, and MPP / Tempo through framework
adapters in AIR Kit.

See [Existing frameworks](/airai/concepts/frameworks) for the full mapping.
