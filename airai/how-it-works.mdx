Whatever the agent is doing — reading a preference, proving a fact, or paying for
something — AIR runs it through the same five stages. The agent never touches raw keys
or data; it receives the *result* of a permitted action, and every stage leaves an
audit trail.

## The five stages

| Stage | What happens | Audit event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Intent** | The user starts a request. The external agent (Butler or Concierge) calls AIR through the AIR AI Plugin (MCP / CLI / APIs); AIR normalizes it into a request. | Request received |
| **2 · Permission** | The AIR Policy Engine decides: allow, deny, revoke, or require approval. It checks pre-authorized [Authorization Flows](/airai/builder-surfaces#authorization-flows) first, and escalates to HITL signers if the action is not covered. | Policy decision |
| **3 · Authorize** | AIR Identity (Agentic) and/or AIR Money (Agentic) authorize against AIR Kit — vault data and credential proofs on the identity side, wallet / account / cards on the money side. | Claim / mandate asserted |
| **4 · Execute** | AIR composes and adapts the request to the relevant standards, then runs identity-related actions, money-related actions, or both. | Action applied |
| **5 · Settle** | External parties confirm — identity verification and fund settlement complete the request. | Result confirmed |

**Cross-cutting — AIR Policy + Audit.** Every stage emits an audit event. Records,
receipts, and revocation history all converge here, surfaced for the user, for
compliance, and for revocation. No sensitive action bypasses consent, policy,
revocation, and audit.

## Worked example — status-matched checkout

A user asks an agent to book a flight to Tokyo and keep it under **\$800**.

<Frame caption="Status-matched checkout — an identity proof (Gold tier) unlocks a member fare that brings the purchase inside the agent's budget, across all five stages with audit cross-cutting. Click to zoom.">
  <img src="/images/airai-status-matched-checkout.png" alt="Status-matched checkout flow — Intent, Permission, Authorize, Execute, Settle with the status match (Gold tier proof unlocks a 15% member fare, $920 to $782 under the $800 budget) and a cross-cutting AIR Audit / Receipts lane" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set budget — Intent">
    "Book the Tokyo flight, keep it under \$800." The agent calls AIR; the request is
    normalized and a budget intent is recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Policy allows — Permission">
    The AIR Policy Engine checks the agent's pre-authorized scope and budget. Allowed
    — no human approval needed for an in-scope request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prove Gold, match budget — Authorize">
    The base fare is **\$920** — over budget. AIR Identity (Agentic) proves the user's
    **Gold tier** loyalty credential — held in their vault, anchored on
    [Moca Chain](/mocachain/index) — on demand. That unlocks a **15% member fare**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply discount, charge — Execute">
    The member fare brings the price to **\$782** — under the \$800 ceiling. AIR Money
    (Agentic) confirms funds and authorizes the charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify tier, settle — Settle">
    The airline verifies the Gold tier proof and settles the **\$782** charge. A
    receipt is emitted to the user.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**The status match.** The tier proof is what makes the purchase achievable. Without it
the fare stays at \$920 — over the ceiling — and the agent cannot transact. This is the
core AIR AI pattern: an **identity proof** unlocks a price that brings a purchase inside
**money's** budget, and **policy + audit** record every step.
</Tip>

## Why this matters for partners

Because every action is shaped the same way, you integrate once and get identity,
money, policy, and audit together — not four separate integrations. The same lifecycle
applies whether the agent is yours (Butler) or you embed AIR inside your own surface
(Concierge).
