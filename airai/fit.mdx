AIR AI is the identity, money, consent, policy, and audit layer for agents. There are
many ways to work with it — this page is the map. Whether you **already know what you're
building** or are **still exploring**, pick the path that matches where you are and
follow it into the details.

<Info>
**The short version.** You keep your agent, your product, and your rails. AIR adds the
permission layer that makes agent actions safe, provable, and revocable — over one
integration instead of N×M bespoke ones.
</Info>

## If you already know what you're building

You're not looking for another agent — you're looking for the layer that lets your agent
(or your users' agents) act safely. Pick the role that fits and follow it into the
details:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="You build a consumer agent or assistant" icon="robot" href="/airai/integration-paths">
    A personal assistant, shopping agent, or travel agent acting for the user. AIR makes
    it a trusted **Butler** — it can carry the user's identity and spend within limits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You build a merchant or enterprise surface" icon="store" href="/airai/integration-paths">
    A checkout, booking flow, or support agent serving your customers. AIR makes it a
    **Concierge** — it can verify eligibility and take payment without holding raw PII.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You build a wallet or signer" icon="wallet" href="/airai/concepts/frameworks">
    AIR composes with your wallet as the user-controlled signing boundary — high-assurance
    approval for high-risk or out-of-scope actions sits with you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You build a payment rail or commerce protocol" icon="money-bill-transfer" href="/airai/concepts/frameworks">
    AIR sits above your rail as the identity + policy + audit layer, routing
    policy-approved authorizations into your settlement path.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You issue identity or credentials" icon="id-badge" href="/airai/identity">
    A standard, KYC provider, or loyalty issuer. AIR exposes agent-callable proof handoff,
    selective disclosure, and freshness / revocation on top of what you issue.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### What you bring vs. what AIR provides

| If you're building… | You bring | AIR provides | Plug-in point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Consumer agent (Butler)** | The agent, its reasoning, its UX, the user relationship. | Scoped identity access, spend mandates, consent + revocation, audit. | Install an [AIR Skill / Plugin](/airai/integration-paths). |
| **Merchant / enterprise surface (Concierge)** | The storefront, booking, or support experience. | Eligibility proofs, purpose-bound data release, payment authorization, merchant-facing receipts. | Build against the [AIR AI SDK](/airai/integration-paths). |
| **Wallet / signer** | The custody and signing UX, key control. | A policy + audit layer that escalates high-risk actions to your signer. | User-controlled signer boundary; see [frameworks](/airai/concepts/frameworks). |
| **Payment rail / protocol** | Settlement, network, instruments. | User-level authorization, credentials, budgets, receipts above the rail. | Framework adapter; see [frameworks](/airai/concepts/frameworks). |
| **Standard / credential issuer** | The schema, issuance, or standard. | Agent-callable proof handoff, selective disclosure, freshness / revocation. | [AIR Identity (Agentic)](/airai/identity). |

## If you're still exploring

You don't need a full agent program to benefit from AIR — and you don't need to predict
the whole agent future to act. The pragmatic reality: **your customers will soon show up
with their own agents** (a ChatGPT or Claude acting on their behalf), whether or not you
have a strategy. The first move is to make your existing surfaces safe for that, then
grow from there.

### Three common first moves

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Make your checkout agent-ready" icon="cart-shopping" href="/airai/how-it-works#worked-example-status-matched-checkout">
    Add a **Concierge** to an existing checkout or booking flow so it can verify
    eligibility (age, KYC, membership) and take payment — without your team holding raw
    PII. Lowest-lift, highest-value entry point for most enterprises.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Let users bring their own agent" icon="user-plus" href="/airai/concepts/agent-taxonomy">
    Accept a user's **Butler** agent into your app with scoped, revocable permissions,
    so it can act for them safely instead of scraping or screen-driving your UI.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Make your credentials agent-usable" icon="id-badge" href="/airai/identity">
    If you already issue identity, loyalty, or KYC credentials via
    [AIR Kit](/airkit/index), expose them for agent-driven proof handoff so any agent can
    prove a user's status without re-verification.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Start with one flow

You don't need to commit to a platform decision. Pick a single high-value flow, prove it
end-to-end in the [sandbox](/airai/integration-paths) against mock partners, and decide
from there. The [status-matched checkout](/airai/how-it-works#worked-example-status-matched-checkout)
is a good first flow — it shows identity, money, and policy working together in one
purchase.

### Questions to find your first use case

- Where do customers **re-verify** something they've already proven elsewhere (age, KYC, tier)?
- Where would an agent **transacting on a user's behalf** save real friction — checkout, rebooking, renewals?
- What data are you **holding today that you'd rather not** — and could replace with a proof?
- Which flow has the clearest **budget and approval rules** an agent could follow?

Any "yes" is a candidate for a first AIR-enabled flow.

## How we work together

AIR is infrastructure, so partnership is integration-first, not a rebuild:

<AirAiAlphaAccess />

- **Compose, don't replace.** AIR uses the existing [AIR Kit](/airkit/index) stack and
  integrates with x402, AP2, Stripe, Visa, Mastercard, MPP / Tempo, Ledger, and the
  Open Wallet Standard. See [Existing frameworks](/airai/concepts/frameworks).
- **One permission model across both relationships.** Whether the agent is yours or
  your users', the same identity / money / policy / audit semantics apply. See
  [Agent taxonomy](/airai/concepts/agent-taxonomy).
- **Start in a sandbox.** Once you have alpha access, prove a flow end-to-end against
  mock partners before any bespoke engineering. See
  [Integration paths](/airai/integration-paths).
