AIR Loyalty connects customer status and rewards through two complementary tracks:

- **Loyalty credentials** prove membership, tier, or achievement. Customers carry them in their AIR Account and present them to participating partners for verification.
- **Loyalty points** represent reward value. A funded campaign defines how customers earn, where they spend, and what happens to unused rewards.

A credential is proof of status, not a live points balance. Your loyalty engine determines qualifying activity and status; the credential and points flows serve different parts of the experience.
