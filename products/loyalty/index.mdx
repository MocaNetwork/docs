AIR Loyalty turns a customer's loyalty status into a credential in their AIR Account. You issue it when your loyalty engine records a tier change or milestone. A partner brand verifies it at the point of benefit and receives only the tier information its program requests, with the customer's approval. You and the partner never need a direct API integration.

Loyalty credentials are AIR credentials, so AIR Loyalty is built on the same schemas, issuance, and verification as [AIR Identity](/products/identity/credentials-flow).

## What you can build

- **Tier credentials:** issue a Gold or Platinum credential and replace it when the tier changes.
- **Milestone badges:** issue a credential when a customer crosses a points, purchase, or visit threshold.
- **Cross-brand benefits:** let partners grant perks based on a tier you issued.
- **Time-boxed status:** set an expiry for seasonal or campaign-based access.

## Before you build

AIR Loyalty uses the setup shared by every AIR product:

1. [Set up your partner account](/get-started/dashboard/account-setup), then configure [SDK authentication](/get-started/authentication/sdk-auth) and your [JWKS endpoint](/get-started/authentication/jwks-endpoint).
2. Design the loyalty credential's [schema](/products/identity/schema-overview).
3. [Issue credentials](/products/identity/issuing-credentials) from your backend.
4. Partners set up [verification](/products/identity/verify) for the benefits they offer.

Then follow the [end-to-end loyalty credential flow](/products/loyalty/loyalty-points-issuance).
