Agentic Commerce lets an agent bring the user's approved identity into a shopping or booking experience. A participating merchant can recognize the membership or eligibility behind a request and return the benefits that customer qualifies for.

The starting point is a clear offer: a member price, a tier benefit, or a discount with defined eligibility. AIR provides the identity and credential-verification flow. The merchant owns the offer rules, and purchase and payment authority are handled separately.

## Member discounts

A customer asks an agent to find a product. They already hold a loyalty credential from an issuer the merchant accepts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The user approves identity access">
    The customer connects the chosen agent and approves access for the task. The integration identifies both the agent and the user it represents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The agent verifies eligibility">
    The agent's backend runs the agreed program for membership or tier evidence. It passes the result and relevant presentation in the format the merchant accepts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The merchant applies the benefit">
    The merchant checks the evidence and returns the eligible price or discount with its terms. The agent can compare that offer against the user's requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The user proceeds under the purchase rules">
    Ordering and payment use their own approvals and supported checkout flow. Permission to verify membership alone does not authorize spending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For example, an accepted Gold-tier credential could qualify a customer for a lower price. The merchant needs the eligibility evidence for that benefit, rather than the customer's full membership history. Configure disclosure for the actual claims required.

## Travel and other entitlements

The same pattern can let a booking agent request a member rate or an upgrade using accepted loyalty status. Preferences such as seat choice help the agent choose options, while credentials establish eligibility for the benefit.

See the [agent booking demo](https://air-bd-v2.netlify.app/demo/agent-booking) for an illustrative journey. A demonstration of checkout does not give an identity binding wallet signing authority; wallet delegation for Web3 use cases is planned separately.

## Relevant offers during discovery

A merchant could make an offer available while an agent is comparing products. Verified, user-approved claims can establish whether the customer qualifies, and the agent can evaluate the offer alongside price, quality, and the user's request.

For a first-purchase discount, agree how first-time eligibility is established and how one-time use is tracked. Do not assume that every required claim already exists as a credential.

Broader offer discovery and merchant-funded campaigns are a product direction. A general advertising marketplace, discovery API, and automated revenue-sharing model are not established by the identity integration described here. Agree who gives consent, which issuers and claims the merchant accepts, and whether it trusts an agreed backend result or checks the presentation itself.
