## Capability summary

See the [platform support matrix](/get-started/platform-matrix) for supported platforms, browsers, and package installs.

Credential **issuance** and **verification** in the client SDK are available on Flutter. Start with [Issue credentials](/get-started/quickstarts/issue-credentials), [Verify credentials](/get-started/quickstarts/verify-credentials), [Issuing credentials](/products/identity/issuing-credentials), and [Verify](/products/identity/verify). [On-demand issuance](/api-reference/issuance-api) works from any backend.

## Authentication on Flutter

On Flutter, sign-in uses `login` with a **Partner JWT** (`authToken`) after `initialize()`. See [User login & sessions](/get-started/user-authentication) (Flutter tab) and [Partner authentication](/get-started/partner-authentication).

## Environment and dashboard

Use the [Developer Dashboard](https://developers.sandbox.air3.com/dashboard) for partner ID, programs, and configuration. Environment values match [Environments](/get-started/environments) (`Environment.sandbox`, `production`, etc.).
