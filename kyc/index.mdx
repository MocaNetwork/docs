Moca Network partners can onboard users with **[Veriff](https://www.veriff.com/) powered by [zkMe](https://www.zk.me)** — a bundled KYC pipeline that produces standard **AIR Kit credentials**. You contract with **zkMe** for this path; no separate KYC vendor procurement is required. **zkMe** is the exclusive Web3 on-chain provider for **Veriff**.

The output is the same credential model as the rest of AIR Kit: users hold attestations in their AIR Account, and verifiers receive **COMPLIANT** or **NON_COMPLIANT** signals via zero-knowledge proofs — not raw identity documents on your servers.

---

## Two ways to run KYC on Moca

| Path | When to use |
|------|----------------|
| **[Veriff powered by zkMe](/kyc/index)** (this section) | You want a turnkey pipeline: document + biometric + AML + optional manual review, operated under zkMe, with no separate KYC vendor contract. |
| **[Identity & Compliance](/solutions/identity)** (bring your own provider) | You already use Jumio, Onfido, Persona, or another provider and only need AIR Kit to turn pass results into portable credentials. |

Both paths issue **AIR Credentials** and support the same verification experience for downstream apps. Choose based on procurement and who operates the verification stack.

---

## What is included

- **Programmatic verification** — including **zkPassport**-style NFC reading of ICAO-compliant electronic passports and national eIDs where supported, with on-device zero-knowledge proof generation so raw chip data does not leave the user device for that step.
- **Manual reverification** — if automated checks cannot reach a decision (poor NFC read, edge-case document, biometric mismatch), the case escalates to human review under the **Veriff powered by zkMe** operator workflow — the same regulatory outcome as a classic eKYC session.
- **AML and sanctions screening** — bundled as part of the regulated identity pipeline (not optional add-ons you must source separately).
- **Face matching and liveness** — standard components of compliant identity verification.
