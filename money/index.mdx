AIR Money gives each user a smart account tied to their AIR Account. Users sign in once and can hold, send, and swap assets without installing a wallet or managing a seed phrase. Your app can sponsor gas, connect the account to existing EVM tooling, and open hosted wallet screens.

## What you can build

| Capability | What it does | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smart accounts | Deploy a chain-specific smart account on the user's first transaction, with deployment gas sponsored by AIR Kit. | [Smart accounts](/airkit/usage/account/smart-account) |
| Wallet functions | Sign messages and send transactions through an EIP-1193 provider. | [Wallet functions](/airkit/usage/account/provider-functions) |
| Wagmi | Use the smart account in React apps with the AIR Kit wagmi connector. | [Wagmi connector](/airkit/usage/account/wagmi) |
| Gas sponsorship | Subsidize user transactions with allowlists, budget caps, and per-user limits. | [Paymaster](/airkit/usage/account/paymaster) |
| Hosted wallet UI | Open swap, on-ramp, send and receive, and transaction history screens inside your app. | [Built-in UI](/airkit/usage/account/builtin-ui) |
| Session keys | Let users approve a scoped permission once, then sign without repeated prompts. | [Session keys](/airkit/usage/account/session-keys) |

Check which networks your partner configuration supports in [Supported chains](/airkit/usage/account/supported-chains).

## Before you build

AIR Money uses the setup shared by every AIR product:

1. [Set up your partner account](/airkit/usage/getting-started) and register your app's domain.
2. [Install](/airkit/usage/installation) and [initialize](/airkit/usage/initialization) AIR Kit.
3. Add [user login](/airkit/usage/user-authentication). A smart account belongs to a signed-in AIR Account.
4. Contact the AIR team to set up gas sponsorship policies and the chains in your partner configuration. See [Paymaster](/airkit/usage/account/paymaster).

For a working login in a few minutes, follow the [account and login quickstart](/airkit/quickstart/index).
